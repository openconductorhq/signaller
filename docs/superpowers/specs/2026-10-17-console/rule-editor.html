Writing your own rule

A manual rule sits in the Rulebook like the built-in ones: drag it to any position, switch it on or off. While you type, the router checks the rule against your last 200 requests.

when text matches /migration|schema|rollback/i
  and tier is sonnet
go anthropic
✓ Valid. In your last 200 requests this would have matched 7, and changed the track for 5 of them.
